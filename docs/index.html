Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Welcome to Steadfast (also identified internally as Istiqamat). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data required to provide a seamless habit-tracking experience. This includes:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google, we collect your email address and a unique user identifier (UID) via Firebase Authentication.
  • App Usage Data: We sync your habit streaks, points, shields, and daily completion logs to our secure Firestore cloud database.
  • Analytics & Prevention of Abuse: We track ad watches and points earned. We collect this data solely to ensure the app is being used ethically, to track genuine players, and to prevent cheating or artificial point manipulation.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for functioning of the Steadfast app:

  • To back up your progress across devices securely.
  • To reward you with in-app points and maintain the app's economy shield.
  • To serve relevant ads via Google AdMob to allow you to earn in-game items (Google may collect device identifiers as per their own privacy policies).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Advertising and Rewards

Steadfast integrates Google AdMob to serve video advertisements. We offer users the option to voluntarily watch these "rewarded ads." In exchange for viewing an ad, users earn virtual in-game items, such as points or shields, which enhance their app experience.

Please note that AdMob may use device identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID) to personalize ads and analyze performance. By interacting with these ads, you consent to AdMob's data collection practices.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase (Auth & Firestore)
  • Google AdMob

You have the absolute right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data from our servers. If you wish to delete your data, please contact us directly via email.
